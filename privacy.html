Life Hub Privacy Policy

Life Hub is a personal app used only by its owner. It connects to the owner's own Google and Microsoft accounts to read and organize his email and read his calendar.

  • Data is used only to show the owner his own information in his private dashboard.
  • Data is stored on the owner's private server and is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • The app never sends email and never deletes it.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: use the support email shown on Life Hub's Google sign-in screen.
